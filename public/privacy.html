Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: email, username, name, profile picture and bio.
  • What you create: posts, reels, stories, comments and messages.
  • Basic technical data needed to run the app, such as your device's notification token if you allow notifications.

How we use it

We use your data to run Twik: to show your content to the people you choose, deliver messages and calls, send notifications and keep the community safe. We do not sell your personal data.

Who can see what

Public accounts can be seen by everyone. Private accounts are only seen by approved followers. Messages are shared only with the people in the chat. You can turn activity status and read receipts off in Settings → Privacy.

Camera, microphone and notifications

We only use the camera and microphone when you take a photo, record a video or make a call, and notifications only if you allow them. You can change this in your device settings.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with our service providers (database and file storage). Calls are sent directly between devices when possible, or through a relay server when needed.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, make your account private, block people, and delete your account at any time in Settings. Deleting your account removes your profile and content.

Contact

Questions? Use Settings → Report a problem inside the app.
